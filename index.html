About Me

I'm a licensed Electronics Engineer (ECE, ECT) based in San Pablo City, Laguna, Philippines, and a 2024 graduate of Laguna State Polytechnic University, licensed in 2025. What draws me to this field is the full loop of building something: designing the circuit, writing the code that drives it, and shipping it somewhere people can actually use it. My undergraduate thesis was a Raspberry Pi based medicine dispensing system with RFID authentication, built for our university clinic. Since then I've kept building through personal projects, all deployed live on Render and Vercel: training and deploying a TensorFlow image classification model, and building IoT dashboards that connect embedded sensors to live web interfaces. I use AI tools throughout these projects to speed up the repetitive parts, so my own effort goes into understanding how each piece actually works, from the circuit to the code to the deployment. I'm interested in Electronics, Software/AI/Data Engineering, Web Development, and Network Engineering (MikroTik certified, with working knowledge of Cisco) — the space where hardware and software come together to actually solve something, and I'm looking for the opportunity to do that at a bigger scale.
